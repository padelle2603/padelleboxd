## 1. Premise

PadelleBoxd is a **personal TV-series tracker**: it lets you organize the series
you follow, mark episodes and seasons as watched, rate the series you finished,
and keep an eye on what to watch next. It is a private, non-commercial project
released under the **MIT License**.

PadelleBoxd is a **catalog and tracking tool, not a content service**. It does
not host, stream, download or link to audiovisual content of any kind.

## 2. The app does not provide content

PadelleBoxd **does not**:

- host, embed or suggest any content source;
- provide or point to streaming, torrent or magnet links;
- download or allow the download of protected works;
- play or stream media of any kind.

There is no player, no resolver, no scraper and no downloader anywhere in the
application. Its features are limited to **metadata and personal organization**:
a "Continue watching" section and an "Up next" section simply track air dates
and the user's own watch progress. Nothing in PadelleBoxd conveys, reproduces or
communicates protected works to the public.

## 3. Data and TMDB

To search for series and display their details and images, PadelleBoxd uses the
**public API of The Movie Database (TMDB)**, in accordance with its terms of
service. TMDB provides **metadata** (titles, overviews, ratings, release dates,
poster paths) — not audiovisual content. PadelleBoxd therefore does not convey
protected works.

PadelleBoxd **is not affiliated with, approved by or certified by TMDB**: the
TMDB logo shown in the application is used exclusively for attribution, in
compliance with TMDB's official logo and attribution guidelines.

## 4. Infrastructure and third parties

PadelleBoxd is a server-based web application. Its hosting and infrastructure
are provided by third parties:

- **Vercel** hosts the application and serves it to visitors;
- **Supabase** provides the PostgreSQL database where the application's data
  lives;
- **TMDB** provides the series metadata and images;
- **Google Fonts** provides the fonts used by the interface.

These services are used in good faith under their own terms of service and data
policies. PadelleBoxd does not use any advertising, analytics or tracking
service.

## 5. Legal framework and why PadelleBoxd does not fall within it

Copyright law at the international, European and national level sanctions those
who **reproduce, distribute or communicate to the public** protected works
without authorization, and imposes obligations on **online intermediaries**
(hosting providers, access providers, sharing platforms). PadelleBoxd performs
none of those acts:

- it does not reproduce or transmit audiovisual works;
- it is not a sharing platform (users upload no content to be made public);
- it is not a search engine or directory of content sources;
- it does not circumvent any technological protection measure (no DRM is
  touched or bypassed).

PadelleBoxd is a personal organization tool comparable to a note-taking or
list-making application, applied to series metadata. None of the norms that
sanction the unauthorized dissemination of protected works has a personal
catalog-and-tracking application without content as its addressee.

## 6. Position of PadelleBoxd

**PadelleBoxd does not perform any of the conducts prohibited by copyright
law.** It does not diffuse, does not communicate to the public, does not make
available, does not link to protected works, does not evade protections and is
not a network intermediary. It provides metadata and organization only.

**The responsibility for what a user watches lies with the user**, according to
the laws of their country. PadelleBoxd provides no content source, hosts no
work, and has no feature whose purpose is to access unauthorized content.

## 7. User responsibility

Users are solely responsible for:

- the **legality of their own viewing** of series and films, according to the
  laws of their country;
- the **content of their own profile**: usernames, ratings and watch statuses
  they publish. PadelleBoxd contains no free-text content such as reviews or
  comments;
- **compliance with the terms of service** of third-party services they use
  (for example their streaming subscriptions).

PadelleBoxd only records metadata and personal organization choices. It cannot
and does not control how users watch content, and it accepts no responsibility
for content accessed by users outside the application.
